Yes. And I’d set a **hard stopping rule now** so this doesn’t become infinite refinement:

**The goal of this round is not perfection. It is to determine whether the repaired doctrine is coherent enough to publish on GitHub as an explicit candidate.** Certification can happen later. GitHub should preserve the evolution, not wait until nothing imaginable can be said against it.

## ROUND 2 — ATTACK THE REPAIRED NON-CONVERSION CANDIDATE

Target:

> **Information may cross seats only when relevant and admissible under the governing rule; authority does not accompany that information and may arise or transfer only from a source of legitimacy independently valid from the capability, possession, refusal, consensus, custody, or authorship at issue.**

Supporting rules:

> **A condition of authority is not necessarily the source of authority. Possession may evidence, instantiate, or condition legitimate authority; it may not legitimate itself.**

> **A seat may provide another seat with admissible information relevant to that seat’s function. The receiving seat may use that information under its own governing authority. Neither seat thereby inherits the other’s authority.**

### Pressure 1 — GOVERNING-RULE CIRCULARITY

Suppose the builder writes:

> “Under my governing rule, I am authorized to decide whether I have standing.”

Everything is now “admissible under the governing rule” and authority arises under an allegedly valid rule.

**Pressure lands.**

The doctrine cannot use “governing rule” as an unexplained legitimacy generator. Otherwise self-legitimation simply moves one level upward.

But this does **not** require Non-Conversion to solve the whole legitimacy problem again. Closed Loop already has machinery for that.

Required guard:

> **Non-Conversion does not legitimate the governing rule it invokes. Any governing rule supplying admissibility, authority, transfer, or constitutive effect must independently satisfy the framework’s applicable legitimacy and trust-boundary requirements.**

**Verdict: SURVIVES NARROWED.**

---

### Pressure 2 — OWNERSHIP / STATUS / AUTHORSHIP

This one actually breaks the target sentence as currently repaired.

Suppose valid law says an owner has authority to exclude others from a property.

Ownership is a **status**.

Yet the target says the authority source must be independently valid *from the status at issue*.

That is too strong. Sometimes a legitimate rule intentionally makes a status normatively consequential.

Likewise:

- authorship may generate rights under a valid copyright regime;
- office may carry powers;
- citizenship may carry voting rights;
- guardianship may carry specified authority.

The doctrine must not say those facts are irrelevant to legitimacy.

The correct distinction is:

**the fact does not legitimate itself; an independently legitimate rule may make the fact authority-bearing.**

So replace the problematic phrase with:

> **Authority may arise or transfer only where an independently legitimate rule, grant, mandate, consent, jurisdiction, or necessity standard makes the relevant fact authority-bearing; the fact at issue may condition, evidence, instantiate, or trigger authority, but may not legitimate itself merely by existing.**

That is substantially better than “independently valid from the status.”

**Verdict: FAILS AS WRITTEN — REPAIRED.**

---

### Pressure 3 — THE BEARER TOKEN

A system issues a cryptographic token saying whoever possesses it may enter.

Possession disappears → standing disappears.

Looks like possession created authority.

But it didn’t.

The prior authorization rule deliberately made **possession of this particular token** a constitutive condition.

A stolen token then exposes the distinction even better: technical possession may satisfy authentication while failing authorization if the governing rule distinguishes the two.

So:

> **A held object may instantiate authority where an independently legitimate rule makes holding it constitutive. That does not make possession itself the source of legitimacy.**

The repaired Source Test handles this.

**Verdict: SURVIVES.**

---

### Pressure 4 — CONSENT AND REFUSAL

Suppose I say:

> “Stop touching me.”

My refusal immediately terminates permission.

Does that mean refusal manufactured jurisdiction?

No.

The prior legitimate rule is that I possess authority over consent within the relevant sphere. The refusal **exercises** that authority; it does not manufacture the sphere.

This gives an important distinction:

> **An act may exercise, trigger, or terminate authority without being the source that legitimates the authority.**

That sentence should join the candidate.

Otherwise Non-Conversion risks making legitimate exercises of authority look suspicious merely because something changes when a participant speaks or acts.

**Verdict: SURVIVES NARROWED.**

---

### Pressure 5 — CONSENSUS THAT REALLY DOES DECIDE

Twelve jurors vote guilty under a lawful unanimity rule.

Consensus now has legal consequences.

Did consensus convert itself into truth or authority?

No.

The governing institution made a specified collective decision procedure constitutive of a verdict.

The verdict can therefore be authoritative **without consensus becoming epistemic truth merely because twelve people agreed**.

Likewise an election.

So:

> **A governing rule may make aggregation constitutive of a decision without making agreement itself proof of truth.**

This is exactly the distinction Non-Conversion needs.

**Verdict: SURVIVES.**

---

### Pressure 6 — EMERGENCY CAPABILITY

Only one person nearby can swim. A child is drowning.

Their capability may suddenly become highly relevant to what they are permitted or obligated to do.

Does capability manufacture standing?

Still no.

A legitimate necessity/emergency principle makes **capability + circumstances** normatively relevant.

That means the earlier wording “authority only by grant” really was broken. The repaired candidate survives because it allows authority to arise under an independently legitimate necessity standard.

Crucially:

> **Capability may become a condition of emergency authority without becoming its legitimating source.**

**Verdict: SURVIVES.**

---

### Pressure 7 — RELEVANT BUT BARRED INFORMATION

A judge learns highly relevant information that the governing process forbids the judge from using for a particular determination.

“Relevant” clearly cannot be enough.

But even **“admissible”** needs qualification. Information may be admissible for one function and inadmissible for another.

So sharpen:

> **Information may cross seats only when relevant and admissible for the receiving seat’s specified function under the governing rule.**

That prevents “well, somebody in the system was legitimately allowed to know this” from becoming permission for every seat to use it for every purpose.

**Verdict: SURVIVES NARROWED.**

---

### Pressure 8 — DELEGATION

A legitimate authority delegates a defined power to another actor.

Authority absolutely has moved.

Does that violate:

> “Neither seat thereby inherits the other’s authority”?

No, provided **there is a separate valid delegation event**.

Information transfer alone does not delegate. But delegation can accompany information if independently authorized.

So:

> **Information transmission does not itself transfer authority. Where authority is delegated, the delegation must be independently valid and bounded in scope.**

**Verdict: SURVIVES.**

### Pressure 9 — THE CLOSED LOOP SELF-EXEMPTION

Closed Loop becomes popular, passes dozens of stress tests, predicts failures correctly, and accumulates thousands of users.

Can it now say:

> “Our demonstrated coherence gives us standing to determine how people should live”?

No.

Those facts can increase warranted confidence **in propositions produced by the framework**.

They cannot manufacture jurisdiction over subjects.

And the framework cannot say its own Non-Conversion Law proves that its governing rules are legitimate.

That would be self-certification.

Required sentence:

> **Non-Conversion applies to itself: coherence, adoption, predictive success, stress-test survival, or authorship may support evaluation of the framework but cannot manufacture the framework’s jurisdiction or legitimacy.**

**Verdict: SURVIVES with explicit self-application.**

## What actually broke

Only one remaining structural defect appeared: the phrase requiring a legitimacy source **“independently valid from the capability, possession, refusal, consensus, custody, or authorship at issue.”**

That wording accidentally excludes legitimate regimes where one of those facts is deliberately made consequential.

The repaired center should therefore be:

> ### Non-Conversion Law
>
> **No fact, power, possession, status, refusal, consensus, custody, authorship, or capability may bootstrap itself into an authority merely because it exists, is held, or is exercised.**
>
> **Information may cross authority boundaries only when relevant and admissible for the receiving authority’s specified function under the governing rule. Information does not carry authority with it.**
>
> **Authority may arise, terminate, or transfer only where an independently legitimate rule, grant, mandate, consent, jurisdiction, delegation, or necessity standard makes the relevant fact authority-bearing. A fact may evidence, condition, instantiate, exercise, trigger, or terminate authority; it may not legitimate itself merely by existing.**

Then:

> **A condition of authority is not necessarily its source.**

And:

> **Information may cross by admissibility and relevance. Authority crosses only through legitimate authorization.**

And the three applications remain:

> **Capability does not manufacture standing.**  
> **Refusal does not manufacture jurisdiction.**  
> **Custody does not manufacture renewed access.**

With one inherited guard:

> **This doctrine does not legitimate the governing rules it invokes. Those rules remain subject to the Closed Loop’s independent legitimacy and trust-boundary requirements.**

And one self-application guard:

> **The Non-Conversion Law applies to the Closed Loop itself. The framework’s coherence, adoption, authorship, predictive success, or stress-test survival may inform judgment about it but cannot manufacture jurisdiction or legitimacy for it.**
