Attack complete. **Ruling: BLOCKED — one load-bearing defect.**

The core survives, but the target sentence does not survive unchanged:

> **“Influence may flow by relevance; authority may flow only by grant.”**

Both halves are too broad.

The first breaks because **relevance is not enough**. Information can be relevant and still be inadmissible, unlawfully acquired, outside scope, privileged, stale, or barred by the governing rule. If relevance alone lets influence cross seats, the evidence seat gets a back door into judgment.

The second breaks because **grant is too narrow**. Legitimate authority can arise through consent, office, jurisdiction, custody, ownership, constitutional rule, delegated mandate, or a valid emergency/necessity rule. Some of those can be described loosely as “grants,” but forcing them all into that word obscures the actual principle.

The surviving rule is stronger:

> **Information may cross seats only when relevant and admissible under the governing rule; authority does not accompany that information and may arise or transfer only from a source of legitimacy independently valid from the capability, possession, refusal, consensus, custody, or authorship at issue.**

That sentence survives much more pressure.

| Pressure | Verdict | What breaks / survives |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Sphere boundary** | **SURVIVES NARROWED** | Refusal cannot create the sphere. The sphere needs an independently legitimate basis. Refusal may then **invoke** or protect authority already present inside it. |
| **2. Record vs. encounter** | **SURVIVES NARROWED** | Record custody, record use, and encounter standing must be separate. Revocation can terminate prospective encounter access without erasing valid history. Custody alone cannot recreate access. |
| **3. Legitimate conversion** | **FAILS AS WRITTEN** | Some possessions are conditions of legitimate authority: a credential, key, commission, capability token. But the authority exists because a prior valid rule makes possession consequential—not because possession bootstraps itself. |
| **4. Seat transformation / delegation** | **SURVIVES NARROWED** | Authority may move between seats, but only through a valid transfer rule. Information crossing seats does not itself transfer authority. |
| **5. Refusal asymmetry** | **SURVIVES NARROWED** | “Refusal cannot manufacture jurisdiction” survives only if refusal can still withhold or terminate consent inside an already legitimate sphere. Otherwise the rule could bulldoze bodily or personal autonomy. |
| **6. Custody asymmetry** | **SURVIVES NARROWED** | Stored evidence may legitimately trigger a new process only where a separate governing rule authorizes that use. **Custody is not use authority. Use authority is not encounter standing.** |
| **7. Consensus** | **SURVIVES NARROWED** | Consensus can evidence the social fact that many people believe X. It can also be a constitutive input where a valid rule says votes decide something. It does not become truth merely through multiplication. |
| **8. Emergency** | **FAILS “GRANT” WORDING** | Real emergencies may generate temporary authority through an independently legitimate necessity standard even where no person has issued a fresh grant. Scope must remain bounded and later auditable. |
| **9. Self-application** | **SURVIVES** | Closed Loop’s coherence, adoption, predictive success, or ability to model someone cannot manufacture jurisdiction over that person. Those facts can support confidence in the model, not authority over the modeled subject. |

The **deepest test also breaks the current wording**. A seat cannot pass information merely because it is relevant. The positive rule has to distinguish **transmission** from **conversion**:

> **A seat may provide another seat with admissible information relevant to that seat’s function. The receiving seat may use that information under its own governing authority. Neither seat thereby inherits the other’s authority.**

That is clean. Evidence can inform judgment without becoming judgment. Judgment can inform legitimate action without automatically authorizing it. Legitimacy can authorize use without becoming evidence that the judgment is true.

There is also a defect in our supposedly clean **test pair**.

We had:

> Remove the possession: does standing survive?  
> Remove the grant: does standing survive?

That fails on **constitutive conditions**. Imagine a valid rule saying only the holder of credential X may exercise authority Y. Remove the credential and authority disappears—but that does **not** prove possession was the legitimating source. The governing rule made possession a condition.

So the repaired test is:

> **Remove the possession. If standing survives, possession was plainly not its source.**
>
> **If standing disappears, remove the independently valid rule that made possession authoritative. If possession alone would still supposedly confer standing, the system has bootstrapped legitimacy and fails.**

That gives us the distinction we needed:

> **Possession may evidence, instantiate, or condition legitimate authority. It may not legitimate itself.**

And that produces an even cleaner Non-Conversion Law:

> ## CANDIDATE — Non-Conversion Law
> **No fact, power, possession, status, refusal, consensus, custody, authorship, or capability may bootstrap itself into a different authority merely because it is held or exercised.**
>
> **Information may cross authority boundaries when relevant and admissible under the governing rule. Authority crosses those boundaries only through an independently valid source or transfer rule.**

Then the three sharp applications remain intact:

**Capability does not manufacture standing.**  
**Refusal does not manufacture jurisdiction.**  
**Custody does not manufacture renewed access.**

And now we can add the general rule they instantiate:

> **A condition of authority is not necessarily the source of authority.**

That was the hole.

So I would **not certify or insert Agency Integrity yet**. But the attack did not kill the Non-Conversion Law. It forced its center narrower and, I think, substantially stronger.

**Final ruling: BLOCKED on the target sentence; core proposition SURVIVES NARROWED.**

Exact unblock:

> **Information may cross seats only when relevant and admissible under the governing rule; authority does not accompany that information and may arise or transfer only from a source of legitimacy independently valid from the capability, possession, refusal, consensus, custody, or authorship at issue.**
