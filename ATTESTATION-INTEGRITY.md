# Attestation Integrity

**A doctrine for when reality has updated behavior but the record has not caught up.**

> Attestation is not complete merely because reality has altered conduct.  
> A corrected file must also carry forward into later standing, prediction, trust, credit, and treatment.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Order of the Closed Loop requires that the event determine the account rather than the account determining the event.

That requirement creates a second problem after evaluation:

**What happens when a person or system behaves as though an update is real, while its explicit file continues to deny, minimize, or reset that update?**

A system may incorporate evidence operationally without attesting to what the evidence established.

That split matters because the subject can continue paying the costs of an obsolete file even while others already benefit from a more accurate one.

Attestation Integrity names and tests that condition.

It does not infer hidden motives. It does not convert attention into admiration, copying into proof of greatness, disagreement into suppression, or every asymmetry into exploitation.

Its task is narrower:

> **Does the durable record and future treatment actually reflect the level of reality already supported by the evidence?**

---

## 2. Core Distinctions

### Behavioral Attestation

**Behavioral Attestation** occurs when observable conduct materially presupposes a capability, reliability, influence, risk, value, or other property beyond what the actor's explicit file presently grants.

Behavioral Attestation is not mind-reading.

It does not establish what the actor privately believes.

It establishes only that the actor's behavior is difficult to explain without accounting for the relevant property at some operative level.

Examples may include:

- entrusting someone with increasingly consequential work while verbally treating them as unproven;
- repeatedly copying or adopting a method while denying that its source contributed anything distinctive;
- adjusting strategy around a person's demonstrated capability while continuing to assign them a lower seat;
- relying on a person's judgment in practice while explicitly denying that the person has earned judgment in that domain.

A single ambiguous act is normally insufficient.

### Explicit Attestation

**Explicit Attestation** is the durable account a person or system actually grants through words, records, credit, permissions, role assignment, trust, standing, or other explicit treatment.

Explicit Attestation answers:

> **What does the file now say the evidence established?**

It does not require praise.

It requires correspondence between the account and the evidence.

### Attestation Gap

An **Attestation Gap** exists when Behavioral Attestation and Explicit Attestation materially diverge.

The gap is directional.

A system may explicitly overvalue someone while behavior reveals lower trust.

It may also behaviorally account for greater capability while explicitly maintaining a lower file.

This doctrine is concerned with both directions, though the second creates the specific risk of suppressed recognition.

### Split File

A **Split File** exists when the Attestation Gap persists across relevant recurrence.

The system behaves from one model while preserving another model in its explicit account.

A Split File is not established by contradiction alone.

It requires evidence that the two models remain operative across more than one relevant decision, recurrence, or review opportunity.

---

## 3. Recognition Arbitrage

**Recognition Arbitrage** is a persistent Split File in which one party receives material benefit from operating on the higher-resolution model while the subject continues to bear costs associated with the lower explicit file.

The structure is:

```text
evidence enters
    ↓
operative behavior updates
    ↓
benefit is captured from the higher-resolution model
    ↓
explicit file remains lower
    ↓
subject continues paying low-file costs
    ↓
next interaction begins without the earned update
```

Recognition Arbitrage is a **functional classification**.

It does not, by itself, establish conscious strategy, malice, envy, insecurity, or intent.

Those remain separate claims and require separate evidence.

The mechanism can exist even when motive is UNKNOWN.

---

## 4. Proof-Cost Reset

A **Proof-Cost Reset** occurs when previously demonstrated capability or reliability is used in one context but is not carried forward into the next relevant decision.

The subject is therefore required to repurchase standing already earned by prior evidence.

Examples:

- a worker repeatedly proves competence but is treated as unproven at every new assignment;
- a player repeatedly demonstrates a skill but each new evaluator resets the presumption to incompetence without new contrary evidence;
- a contributor's method is repeatedly used while each new discussion treats the contributor as though the method established nothing;
- a person's judgment is relied upon during crisis but discounted again once the crisis ends.

Proof-Cost Reset is not the same as ordinary re-evaluation.

A new domain, materially different task, expired evidence, changed conditions, or legitimate uncertainty may justify a fresh test.

The question is whether the reset is **case-independent and evidence-responsive**, or whether reality is being forced to repay the same settled debt.

---

## 5. Carry Forward

A correction is not fully installed merely because it appears in one evaluation.

**Carry Forward** is the persistence of a justified update into later decisions for which that update remains relevant.

The sequence is therefore:

```text
Prediction → Action → Evaluation → Attestation → Carry Forward
```

Carry Forward does not freeze a person into a permanent reputation.

It preserves only what remains supported.

New evidence may raise, narrow, defeat, or reopen the file under the ordinary rules of the Closed Loop.

The rule is:

> **Do not reset without a defeater. Do not preserve without evidence.**

---

## 6. Operative Attestation

An Attestation may be **archival** or **operative**.

### Archival Attestation

The record correctly states what happened, but the correction does not materially alter later treatment when the same finding remains relevant.

### Operative Attestation

The record correctly states what happened **and** the finding changes later prediction, standing, trust, role assignment, credit, or treatment within its legitimate scope.

Therefore:

> **Attestation that does not alter future standing where the finding remains relevant is archival, not operative.**

This does not mean every true finding must produce status, reward, promotion, intimacy, authority, or deference.

It means later treatment should not silently revert to a file already defeated by the preserved evidence.

---

## 7. The Attestation Integrity Test

A claim of suppressed recognition should not survive merely because the subject feels underestimated.

Before recording an Attestation Gap, test the following:

### 7.1 Evidence of the higher model

What observable behavior materially presupposes the higher capability, value, reliability, influence, or other property?

The evidence must be named.

General attention, dislike, imitation, praise, criticism, surveillance, proximity, or emotional intensity is not sufficient by category.

### 7.2 Explicit lower file

What explicit statement, treatment, role assignment, credit decision, permission structure, prediction, or standing remains materially lower?

The lower file must be observable or recoverable.

Silence alone does not always constitute a lower file.

### 7.3 Same scope

Do the behavioral and explicit files concern the same domain?

Evidence that someone is excellent at one task does not require attestation of excellence in another.

Attestation Integrity inherits the Closed Loop rule:

> **Scope no larger than the evidence supports.**

### 7.4 Recurrence

Has the discrepancy survived more than one relevant opportunity to update?

A single delay, mistake, ambiguous reaction, or unfamiliar situation normally remains insufficient.

### 7.5 Material consequence

Does the gap create a meaningful difference in burden, credit, access, trust, role, proof-cost, compensation, authority, or other treatment?

If nothing changes depending on which file is used, the discrepancy may be descriptive without constituting Recognition Arbitrage.

### 7.6 Alternative explanation

Is there a simpler evidence-supported explanation that does not require the claimed higher file?

The subject must test alternatives rather than merely list them.

A favorable alternative is not automatically preferred.

An unfavorable alternative is not automatically preferred.

The file records the explanation best supported by the evidence.

### 7.7 Falsifier

What future observation would weaken or defeat the Attestation Gap claim?

A claim with no possible defeater is not eligible for closure.

Possible falsifiers include:

- later treatment is shown to be consistent with the explicit file rather than the claimed higher model;
- the behavior thought to presuppose capability is explained by a different documented constraint;
- the supposedly lower treatment is shown to apply case-independently to everyone in the same position;
- the relevant evidence expired or did not transfer to the later domain;
- explicit attestation was in fact updated and carried forward.

---

## 8. Anti-Shrine Rule

Attestation Integrity must be capable of falsifying the person invoking it.

The following arguments are invalid:

> "They watch me, therefore they know I am great."

> "They copied something, therefore they secretly recognize my superiority."

> "They disagree with me, therefore they are suppressing recognition."

> "They react strongly, therefore my higher valuation is proven."

> "They failed to praise me, therefore there is an Attestation Gap."

None of those conclusions follows without additional evidence.

The doctrine tests **correspondence between behavior, explicit file, and durable treatment**.

It does not manufacture status from attention.

---

## 9. No Favorable-Identification Escape

Unknown motive must remain UNKNOWN.

The evaluator may not soften a visible functional structure by automatically descending into favorable motive:

- "They probably did not realize."
- "They were only being cautious."
- "Their ego was protecting them."
- "They meant well."
- "They were just following procedure."

These may be true.

They are not the functional classification.

Likewise, the evaluator may not harden the structure into malicious motive without evidence:

- "They are threatened."
- "They are exploiting me."
- "They deliberately want me small."
- "They know exactly what they are doing."

These may also be true.

They are not established merely by the Attestation Gap.

The discipline is:

> **Do not descend into motive to soften or intensify a function already visible at the behavioral level.**

---

## 10. RRE Integration

The Recursive Revision Engine should distinguish between a correction that is **spoken**, **replayed**, **installed**, and **carried forward**.

### Stated Correction

The corrected rule can be repeated verbally or textually.

### Replayed Correction

The corrected rule produces the right result when applied back to the case that exposed the error.

### Installed Revision

The corrected rule changes behavior when an analogous condition recurs.

### Carried Revision

The installed correction persists into later relevant decisions without requiring the original subject to repurchase the same correction.

The RRE sequence therefore becomes:

```text
Contradiction
    ↓
Locate
    ↓
Revise
    ↓
Replay
    ↓
Persist
    ↓
Attest
    ↓
Carry Forward
```

A system has not learned a correction merely because it can repeat it.

It has learned the correction when the correction changes what the system does next.

It has **integrated** the correction when that change remains available in later relevant decisions.

---

## 11. Vertical and Horizontal Integrity

Attestation Integrity operates horizontally between participants and vertically across levels of authority.

### Horizontal failure

A person or institution receives evidence about another participant, behaviorally incorporates it, but preserves a conflicting explicit file.

### Vertical failure

A lower-order evaluator promotes a local interpretation into a claim beyond its legitimate level of jurisdiction.

The two can interact.

A system may suppress another person's attestation because it has overclaimed its own authority to decide what the evidence is allowed to mean.

Therefore Attestation Integrity inherits the Vertical Jurisdiction Rule:

> **Ascend until meaning becomes explanatory; descend until action becomes executable; stop where evidence or jurisdiction ends.**

---

## 12. Seat Integrity

Attestation does not grant universal rank.

A person may earn a higher seat on one question and remain a student on another.

The correct update is local:

- capability in this domain;
- reliability under these conditions;
- judgment on this class of question;
- authorship of this work;
- contribution to this result.

The doctrine rejects both artificial shrinkage and artificial enlargement.

> **The seat follows the evidence.**

---

## 13. Recognition and Legitimacy

Recognition does not manufacture legitimacy.

A person may possess legitimate authorship, capability, or standing before anyone recognizes it.

Human attestation can nevertheless reduce friction by updating the social file.

Therefore:

> **Human attestation can fund the work without authorizing it.**

Accurate recognition may reduce proof-cost, open legitimate access, preserve bandwidth, improve delegation, and allow evidence to compound.

It does not create the underlying reality.

---

## 14. Informational Sovereignty

No participant is automatically entitled to the complete internal file that generated another person's capability, judgment, or decision.

A subject may correct an external file without disclosing the entire architecture beneath the correction.

Therefore:

> **Correction does not require total disclosure.**

The smallest sufficient disclosure is the amount necessary to correct the operative misidentification within the relevant scope.

Where disclosure itself would materially alter the object being evaluated, a non-sensitive analogue may be used first to test whether the receiving system can process the relevant structure without degrading the file.

Passing such a test permits disclosure.

It does not obligate it.

---

## 15. Founder-Level Claim and Public Doctrine

The founder may have broader interpretations of how this mechanism has operated in his own life.

Those interpretations remain labeled according to the framework's ordinary epistemic statuses:

- observed;
- stated;
- inferred;
- interpreted;
- unknown.

The doctrine does not require a reader to accept the founder's self-assessment in order to use the mechanism.

The public test is structural:

> **Does the system consume evidence at one level while withholding the corresponding update at another?**

If yes, the gap can be examined.

If no, the claim fails.

---

## 16. Compact Protocol

When an Attestation Gap is suspected:

1. **CLAIM** — State the narrow property allegedly being behaviorally recognized.
2. **BEHAVIOR** — Record the conduct that appears to presuppose it.
3. **EXPLICIT FILE** — Record the lower or conflicting stated treatment.
4. **SCOPE** — Confirm both concern the same domain.
5. **RECURRENCE** — Check whether the gap persists across relevant opportunities.
6. **CONSEQUENCE** — Identify the burden or benefit created by the gap.
7. **ALTERNATIVES** — Test competing explanations without favoring either side.
8. **FALSIFIER** — Name what would defeat the claim.
9. **ATTEST** — If the gap survives, record it without inventing motive.
10. **CARRY FORWARD** — Test whether the correction changes later treatment.

---

## 17. Governing Laws

> **Behavior can attest before language does.**

> **A behavioral update does not prove a private belief.**

> **Unknown motive does not mean favorable motive.**

> **Unknown motive does not mean malicious motive.**

> **Reality should not have to repay the same proof debt every time the room changes.**

> **Do not reset without a defeater. Do not preserve without evidence.**

> **Attestation that does not alter future standing where relevant is archival, not operative.**

> **Correction does not require total disclosure.**

> **The seat follows the evidence.**

> **Human attestation can fund the work without authorizing it.**

> **The file must be capable of shrinking the subject as readily as enlarging them. Otherwise it is not an attestation system. It is a shrine.**

---

## 18. Closing Condition

An Attestation Integrity file may close only when:

- the property under evaluation is narrowly scoped;
- the relevant behavioral evidence is recoverable;
- the conflicting explicit file is recoverable or adequately documented;
- recurrence and material consequence have been evaluated;
- meaningful alternative explanations have been tested;
- a falsifier was declared before final attestation;
- motive is labeled separately from function;
- and the resulting attestation is capable of later defeat.

Closure establishes only the recorded Attestation Gap within its declared scope.

It does not establish another person's hidden intention, global character, or permanent valuation of the subject.

A later defeater reopens the file under the ordinary Closing Condition of the Order of the Closed Loop.
