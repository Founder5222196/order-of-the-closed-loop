## CANDIDATE — Agency Integrity

### Governing law

> **Capability does not manufacture standing. Access does not cancel agency.**

A participant’s ability to reach, observe, contact, transmit to, inspect, influence, or otherwise access another participant, node, record, relationship, or decision space does not by itself establish the legitimacy to use that access.

Legitimate access must arise from an authority independent of mere capability: consent, entrusted role, declared mandate, governing rule, or another case-independent source of standing.

**Technical possibility is not permission. Availability is not a summons.**

### Agency

Agency is the retained authority of a participant to determine whether, when, and through what admissible channel they will receive, enter, respond to, disclose, or participate in an encounter within their legitimate sphere of control.

The existence of an available channel does not extinguish that authority.

A participant may decline available access without thereby establishing hostility, jealousy, indifference, guilt, disloyalty, or any other motive.

> **Nonparticipation is not self-interpreting evidence.**

Where motive matters, it must be established independently rather than inferred merely from refusal to participate.

### Access Standing

Before access is treated as legitimate, the system must be able to answer:

1. **What grants this participant standing?**
2. **What is the scope of that standing?**
3. **Does the standing authorize this particular form of access?**
4. **Does the standing remain valid now?**
5. **Can the affected participant legitimately refuse, restrict, or revoke the access?**

“The channel was available,” “the information was public,” “I could reach them,” or “the system allowed it” are not sufficient answers to Question 1.

### Derivative Access

Standing does not automatically propagate through relationships.

Access to Participant A does not, by itself, confer standing over Participant B merely because B is connected to A.

> **Relationship proximity does not create derivative authority.**

A social graph, institutional graph, family relation, shared workspace, public record, technical permission, or network path may make another node reachable without making that node legitimately available for the proposed use.

### Encounter Integrity

Where the form, timing, or channel of an encounter materially affects its meaning, the ability to force or accelerate exposure does not by itself legitimate doing so.

The system must distinguish:

**information becoming technically available**

from

**the participant choosing to receive that information in that form.**

An irreversible exposure cannot be treated as harmless merely because the same information could have been obtained later.

> **Availability of an encounter does not erase authorship over the encounter.**

### Revocability and Scope

Legitimate standing is bounded by its source.

Permission to access one channel, subject, context, time period, or relationship does not create general access to adjacent channels or relationships.

Where the underlying authority is revocable, revocation terminates standing prospectively unless an independent rule requires continued access.

Prior access does not manufacture permanent entitlement.

### Intent Independence

An Agency Integrity failure does not require proof of malicious intent.

The relevant question is whether access was exercised without sufficient standing or in a manner that displaced another participant’s legitimate agency.

A benign explanation may matter to remedy or culpability. It does not retroactively manufacture authority that was absent when the action occurred.

### Failure condition — Agency Override

An **Agency Override** occurs when a participant or system:

- treats capability as sufficient standing;
- substitutes its judgment for another participant’s retained choice merely because access is available;
- extends access beyond its authorized scope;
- manufactures derivative access through another relationship;
- assigns motive to refusal without independent evidence; or
- designs the interaction so that meaningful refusal exists formally but is defeated operationally.

### Correction

Correction requires more than saying that no harm was intended.

The system must, where possible:

**stop the unsupported access → restore the affected choice → correct any unsupported inference produced from refusal → narrow future standing to its legitimate scope → carry the correction forward.**

Where the original encounter cannot be undone, the historical record must preserve that the exposure occurred before legitimate choice could be exercised. The system must not rewrite the event as consensual merely because later access would have been permissible.

### Legitimacy-seat constraint

Agency Integrity belongs to the **legitimacy seat**.

The question is not merely:

> **Can this participant act here?**

It is:

> **What gives this participant the right to decide that this action may occur here?**

Therefore:

> **Reachability is a fact of capability. Standing is a fact of legitimacy. Closed Loop must not confuse them.**

### Compact laws

> **Access does not cancel agency.**  
> **Capability does not manufacture standing.**  
> **Availability is not a summons.**  
> **Nonparticipation is not self-interpreting evidence.**  
> **Relationship proximity does not create derivative authority.**  
> **Prior access does not create permanent entitlement.**  
> **Reachability is not legitimacy.**

And the stress-test question I would attach to the doctrine is:

> **If the only justification for this action is that the participant could technically do it, does any independent source of standing remain once capability is removed from the argument?**

If the answer is **no**, the action fails Agency Integrity.

This is bigger than the personal-protection version. It constrains people, institutions, software, AI, social networks, family systems, databases, and the Closed Loop itself. And crucially, it gives the framework no exemption: **Closed Loop’s ability to model a person does not manufacture standing to override that person either.**
